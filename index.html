eOS — Eksponentni Operacijski Sistem — Exponential Operating System

Dolgoročni raziskovalno-razvojni projekt: okolje, ki povezuje AI agente, klasične optimizacijske metode in simulacije kvantnih algoritmov za delo z velikimi kombinatoričnimi prostori, na primer razporejanje virov in finančno analitiko. Projekt je v fazi prototipov in javnih demonstracij. A long-term research and development project: an environment that combines AI agents, classical optimization methods, and simulations of quantum algorithms for working with large combinatorial spaces, such as resource scheduling and financial analytics. The project is at the stage of prototypes and public demonstrations.

Cilj projektaProject goal

Preveriti, kje hibridni pristopi (AI, klasična optimizacija in kvantne simulacije) prinesejo merljive prednosti pri konkretnih praktičnih problemih, ter rezultate objaviti s ponovljivimi meritvami in odprto kodo. To test where hybrid approaches (AI, classical optimization, and quantum simulation) bring measurable benefits on concrete practical problems, and to publish the results with reproducible measurements and open-source code.
